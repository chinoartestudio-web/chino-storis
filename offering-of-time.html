The Story Behind the Artwork

Time, memory, and transformation are recurring elements in the artistic universe of Héctor "Chino" Pérez.

In The Offering of Time, a young figure holds a fish, inviting the viewer to reflect on the relationship between life, time, and the mysteries of existence.

This original artwork belongs to the Crónidas series.
